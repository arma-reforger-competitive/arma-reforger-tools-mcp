- **name**: Snap and Orient Entities to Terrain
- **editor**: world
- **descr**: Snaps and orients selected entities to terrain (not "ground" below, terrain only)
- **file**: `enfusion://ScriptEditor/scripts/WorkbenchGame/WorldEditor/SCR_SnapAndOrientToTerrainPlugin.c`
- **shortcut**: [Ctrl+PageDown]
**Snap and Orient Entities to Terrain** is a plugin adding a shortcut to, as its name implies, snap all selected entities to the terrain.

**Important:** This plugin snaps entities to the **terrain**, not to any surface or another entity.
To snap to anything below, use World Editor's [Ctrl+Down] shortcut (this shortcut does not orient objects to the normal, however).
