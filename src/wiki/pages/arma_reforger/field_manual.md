**RTFM**
**R**ead **T**he **F**ield **M**anual.

The **Field Manual** is the in-game manual providing detailed, illustrated information about game modes ([Arma Reforger:Conflict](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Conflict), [Arma Reforger:Game Master](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Game_Master)), gameplay (movement, commanding, etc), and equipment.

It is accessible through **Main Menu > Field Manual** or **Pause Menu > Field Manual**.

**Note:** See also [Arma Reforger:Field Manual Modding](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Field_Manual_Modding).
