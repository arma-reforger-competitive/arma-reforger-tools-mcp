## Start with the Game

[Video: Quickstart Guide for Arma Reforger](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UPsSCyrOllE)

- Read the [Boot Camp](https://reforger.armaplatform.com/news/boot-camp) article - useful both for beginners' boarding and veteran users to be up-to-date on the game's many aspects!
- Read the in-game [Field Manual](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Field_Manual) (**Main Menu > Field Manual** or **Pause Menu > Field Manual**)
- Set your Settings:
  - Set your graphical preferences (**Main Menu > Settings > Video**)
  - Set your controls (**Main Menu > Settings > Controls**)

then…

## Play in Single Player

### Tutorial

The tutorial is available from the main menu through **Play > Tutorial** (scrolling through the entries may be required).

### Scenarios

**Resistance Ops** has been introduced with Arma Reforger 1.1.0; see its [Update News](https://reforger.armaplatform.com/news/update-march-13-2024) on the official website.
See also the [Workshop](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Workshop) to find and download community-made Single Player Scenarios!

## Playing in Multiplayer

The **Multiplayer** main menu tile offers an interface to choose from various servers - from Official to Community-hosted ones.

## Use Mods

The **Workshop** main menu tile opens the [Arma Reforger:Workshop](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Workshop) that hosts all game-accessible community-created content - missions, assets, features.

Download and activate mods from the Workshop to expand the game's possibilities!
